ON YOUR HOME NETWORK

A window into your PC.

Open a folder in Lumen on your PC, then choose Share on network. Connect here using the address and pairing key shown there.

Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Use current Chrome or Edge and allow local-network access when prompted. If your browser blocks the connection, open the PC address directly in your browser. Installation is available from this HTTPS site; your PC must stay on with sharing enabled.
